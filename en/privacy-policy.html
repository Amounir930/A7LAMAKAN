Privacy Policy

At Ahla Makan for Recruitment & Domestic Services, we prioritize the privacy and security of our website visitors. This Privacy Policy details the types of personal information we collect, and how we use and protect it.

1. Data We Collect

We may collect the following information when you interact with our website:

  • Contact Information: Such as name, phone number, and email when you reach out to us via WhatsApp links or direct phone calls.
  • Technical Usage Data: Such as IP addresses, browser types, visited pages, and time spent on the site to help improve your browsing experience.

2. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies (such as Google Ads tracking and Google Analytics) to analyze website traffic and deliver personalized advertisements relevant to your interests based on your visits to our site.

You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and develop the domestic helper recruitment and home services you request.
  • To communicate with you and respond to your questions and requirements.
  • To optimize website performance and enhance the overall user experience.
  • To deliver relevant, customized advertisements via the Google Ads network and track ad campaign performance.

4. Data Protection and Sharing

We are committed to implementing appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure. We do not sell or lease your personal information to third parties.

5. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to align with technical changes or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
